What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is the process of optimizing your website to rank higher on search engines like Google. It helps increase organic traffic and visibility.

  • On Page SEO
  • Off Page SEO
  • Technical SEO

1. Use the Right Keywords

Choosing the right keywords is the first step in SEO. Use keywords like free seo audit, seo guide, and check free seo score naturally in your content.

2. Optimize Meta Title & Description

Meta tags play a major role in ranking and click-through rate.

  • Use main keyword in title
  • Write attractive description
  • Keep it clear and relevant

3. Improve Website Speed

A fast-loading website improves user experience and SEO ranking.

  • Compress images
  • Use fast hosting
  • Remove unnecessary code

5. Write High Quality Content

Content is the most important part of SEO.

  • Write unique content
  • Use proper headings (H1, H2, H3)
  • Focus on user intent
  • Write at least 800+ words

6. Use Internal Linking

Internal linking helps search engines understand your website structure and improves ranking.

7. Make Your Website Mobile Friendly

Most users visit websites on mobile devices, so your website must be responsive and fast.

8. Fix Technical SEO Issues

Technical SEO ensures that your website is properly crawled and indexed.

  • Fix broken links
  • Add sitemap.xml
  • Use robots.txt
  • Check indexing issues
